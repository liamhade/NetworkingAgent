UI redesign drafts

Five design directions for the Networking Agent. Each folder has a working results page, a search page, a running-search state and a README with the reasoning behind it. All of them use the same real search (Tracksuit, Two Sigma, Citadel…), so you can compare like for like. The research behind them is in _research/ui_audit.md and _research/design_research.md.

1 · Field Notes

Editorial and warm · Linen palette · Fraunces + Source Sans 3

Reads like a written briefing: a headline sentence, a "Start here" band of three best moves, then collapsible company sections with one-line previews. Includes a progress panel and a jump-to rail.

2 · Command Center

Dense pro tool · Midnight dark + Harbor light · Space Grotesk + IBM Plex

A list on the left and a details pane on the right. Pick a company or person to see its details. Has keyboard shortcuts (j/k, Ctrl+K), a compact/comfortable toggle and a dark/light toggle.

ResultsSearchRunningLibraryREADME

3 · Pipeline

Kanban tracker · Pop palette · Plus Jakarta Sans

Results as a job-hunt funnel: New → Shortlisted → Applied/Connected, with one row per company. Cards stay small and details open in a drawer. You can switch to a plain list. "Following up" is a proposed new status.

ResultsSearchRunningLibraryREADME

4 · Company Map

Relationship-first · Harbor palette · Manrope + Public Sans

Closest to today's layout, but cleaned up. Companies are collapsed by default, with their jobs and people side by side when opened. There's a "Jump to company" index, a "Group by Company / Type" switch, and links like "Message Keniel about AI Engineer – GTM".

ResultsSearchRunningLibraryREADME

5 · Calm Focus

Guided, low-density · Sage palette · Inter Tight + Inter

For users who feel overwhelmed: one tab at a time, large cards, "Show 5 more", a "Top picks" band and a progress ring that fills as you pin, apply and connect. The running-search view walks through the phases in friendly language.
