How We Build Data Pipelines

At Acme, we’re building the data platform that powers analytics for thousands of customers. This post explains how our pipelines ingest events, validate them, and load them into the warehouse every few minutes.

Our team uses Airflow for orchestration and dbt for transformations. Each pipeline is owned by a small squad with a clear on-call rotation, and every model has tests that run before deployment.

Validation

Events are validated against a schema registry. Bad events are quarantined and reported to the owning team, who can replay them once the producer is fixed. This keeps the warehouse clean without dropping data.
